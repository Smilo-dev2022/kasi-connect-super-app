# iKasiLink - One Page Fact Sheet

## 🎯 **What We Do**
**The Township Super-App** - Secure chat, digital stokvels, local events, and business discovery for South African townships.

## 💡 **The Problem**
- 13.8M township residents lack integrated digital community tools
- Stokvels use outdated cash-only systems
- Local events and businesses are hard to discover
- Communities are digitally fragmented

## 🚀 **Our Solution**
**All-in-one platform** combining:
- **Secure Chat:** Ward-based messaging with encryption
- **Digital Stokvels:** Group savings with digital wallet
- **Local Events:** Community event discovery and RSVP
- **Business Directory:** Township business support

## 📊 **Market Opportunity**
- **Market Size:** R2.3T informal economy
- **Target Users:** 13.8M township residents
- **Stokvel Members:** 2.5M active members
- **Smartphone Penetration:** 85%+ in urban areas

## 💰 **Business Model**
- **Freemium:** Basic features free, premium R29/month
- **Transaction Fees:** 1.5-3% on financial transactions
- **Business Services:** Premium listings and promotion
- **Revenue Target:** R45M by Year 3

## 🏆 **Why We'll Win**
1. **Township-First:** Built specifically for local needs
2. **Integrated Platform:** All community needs in one app
3. **Offline Capable:** Works with limited internet
4. **Local Data:** POPIA compliant, data stays in SA
5. **No Ads:** Community-focused, not profit-driven
