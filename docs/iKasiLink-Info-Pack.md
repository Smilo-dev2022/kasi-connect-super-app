# iKasiLink - The Township Super-App
## Information Pack & Elevator Pitch

---

## 🎯 **What is iKasiLink?**

**iKasiLink** is a comprehensive township super-app that brings communities together through secure chat, group savings (stokvels), local events, and business discovery - all in one verified, safe platform designed specifically for South African townships.

### **The Problem We Solve**
- **Fragmented Communication:** Township residents struggle to connect with their communities
- **Limited Digital Financial Services:** Stokvels and savings groups lack modern digital tools
- **Missed Local Events:** Community events are poorly promoted and hard to discover
- **Untapped Local Businesses:** Township businesses struggle with visibility and customer reach

---

## 🌟 **Core Features**

### **1. Secure Community Chat**
- End-to-end encrypted messaging
- Ward/area-based group chats
- Media sharing (photos, voice messages)
- Message reactions and replies
- Offline message sync

### **2. Digital Stokvel & Savings**
- Join existing stokvels or create new ones
- Track contributions and withdrawals
- Secure wallet with PIN protection
- Transaction history and receipts
- Group financial management tools

### **3. Local Events & Discovery**
- Discover community events and activities
- RSVP with reminders
- QR code check-in system
- Event creation and management
- Location-based event recommendations

### **4. Township Business Directory**
- Search and discover local businesses
- Business profiles with contact info
- Ratings and reviews
- Special offers and promotions
- Support local township economy

### **5. Safety & Verification**
- Verified user profiles
- Community-driven content moderation
- No ads, no data selling
- Data stays in South Africa
- Built-in safety reporting

### **Technology Stack:**
- **Frontend:** React Native (iOS/Android), React (Web)
- **Backend:** Node.js, Python FastAPI, Express.js
- **Database:** PostgreSQL, Redis, SQLite
- **Search:** Typesense
- **Infrastructure:** Docker, AWS, Nginx
- **Security:** End-to-end encryption, JWT authentication
