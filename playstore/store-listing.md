The Township Super-App for Chat, Money & Community

**Full Description:**
iKasiLink is the all-in-one township super-app that brings your community together. Connect, chat, save money, and discover local events - all in one safe, verified space.

🌟 **Key Features:**
• **Secure Chat** - End-to-end encrypted messaging with your community
• **Stokvel Savings** - Join group savings with built-in wallet features  
• **Local Events** - Discover and RSVP to community gatherings
• **Business Directory** - Find and support local township businesses
• **Safety First** - Verified users and content moderation
• **Offline Ready** - Works even with limited internet

🎯 **Perfect For:**
• Township residents wanting to stay connected
• Community groups and stokvels
• Local business owners
• Event organizers
• Anyone building stronger communities

💬 **Chat Features:**
- Group messaging with your ward/area
- Media sharing (photos, voice messages)
- Message reactions and replies
- Online/offline status

💰 **Financial Features:**
- Join existing stokvels or create your own
- Track contributions and withdrawals
- Secure wallet with PIN protection
- Transaction history

🎉 **Community Features:**
- Discover local events and activities
- RSVP and get reminders
- QR code check-in at events
- Share events with friends

🔒 **Privacy & Security:**
- Your data stays in South Africa
- End-to-end encryption for messages
- No ads, no data selling
- Community-driven moderation

Built by South Africans, for South Africans. Join thousands of users already connecting their communities with iKasiLink.
